What stays on your device

Athar saves familiar words, your review list, answers, round times and results, lesson history, and where you left off. These records let you resume a lesson and help Athar choose words for practice.

Your language, transliteration, autoplay, interface sounds, opponent pace and motion preferences are also stored on your iPhone or iPad. Northbyte does not operate a server that holds your learning profile.

Audio and data use

Arabic pronunciation recordings, images and text are included in the app. Playback requires no microphone access. Athar does not record your voice.

The app has no ads, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not receive or sell your lesson history. You can manage Apple’s system diagnostics in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on the device between lessons. To remove all local Athar data, choose Delete App in iOS or iPadOS. Offload App keeps your documents and data.

Depending on your settings, a device backup may include Athar data. You can manage older backups separately in iCloud or on your computer. Restoring a backup can bring back earlier progress.
